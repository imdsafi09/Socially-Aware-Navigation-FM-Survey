# T10 - Open challenges and future directions

All counts from 10_PreWriting/QUANTITATIVE_FINDINGS.md (direct n = 45; cut-off 2026-10-19).

| Open challenge | Evidence from corpus (exact counts) | Why it matters | Current partial solutions | Direction suggested by the reviewed work | Evidence that would establish progress |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Grounding offline social reasoning | 9/45 direct papers never couple FM output to motion; 6/45 have offline evidence only | Offline accuracy does not imply safe or social motion | Candidate selection and cost shaping (D2/D3) | Closed-loop evaluation of decision models through an executable interface | Same model: offline score vs closed-loop social metrics |
| Human-centred evidence | Human evaluation of robot behaviour in 5/45 (3/45 co-present); 0/5 report inferential statistics on behavioural ratings; 3/45 report statistical testing of any result | EXP-family claims (comfort, legibility) remain weakly validated | Small questionnaires (VLM-Social-Nav, PAD, LE-Nav) | Co-present user studies with validated instruments | Reported sample size, statistical test and effect size |
| Real, unscripted humans and open-world deployment | Unscripted humans in 1/45; open-world deployment 0/45 | Staged participants know the experiment; public reactions are untested | Campus and public-space deployments (GSON, G2-Nav, Walk With Me) | Long-duration deployments in public spaces | Hours / km of operation; interventions; incident rates |
| Safety separation for end-to-end models | FM-independent safety guard in 1/7 direct-action systems; 21/45 overall | Direct action removes the classical safety envelope | CBF / reflex layers around VLM costs (GSON, G2-Nav) | Shielded VLA policies; formal or runtime verification | Collision and near-collision under adversarial / dense scenes |
| Uncertainty-aware social decisions | Explicit uncertainty 0/45; partial 9/45 | Overconfident social judgements in ambiguous scenes | Sampling, voting, GPR variance | Calibrated FM uncertainty propagated to the planner | Calibration error; abstention / fallback rates |
| Latency and staleness | Numeric runtime in 23/45; asynchronous designs 9/45 | Decisions arrive after people have moved | Slow/fast loops, streaming latents (SPARK-VLN), distillation | End-to-end latency measured on robot hardware with moving people | Staleness-aware benchmarks (realistic asynchronous mode) |
| Comparable evaluation | 338 metric definitions; incompatible definitions of success, collision and PSV; SNEI used in 9/45 with different subsets | Results cannot be compared or pooled | Shared benchmarks (SocialNav-SUB, LISN-Bench, SONG, DPed-VLN) | Closed-loop social benchmarks with fixed metric definitions and reported data subsets | Cross-paper leaderboards with fixed splits |
| Neglected social capabilities | Crowd (SPA4) evaluated in 1/45 (pre-FM 11/40); prediction (HUM1) 0/45; legibility (EXP2) 0/45 | FM work rarely measures capabilities central to earlier paradigms, although it addresses them | Prediction coupled with FM path selection or navigation graphs (Etiquette, HCSG) | FM reasoning combined with prediction-based planning | Dense-crowd and legibility metrics alongside norm metrics |
| Validating FM-inferred norms | R3 (FM infers norms) papers evaluating any normative capability 7/25; instruction-following evaluations using an R4 role 7/8 | The FM's own judgement of what is appropriate is seldom measured | Multi-action labels (MAction); human answer distributions (SocialNav-SUB) | Reference judgements of appropriate behaviour collected from people for the same scenes | Agreement of FM-selected behaviour with human appropriateness ratings, in closed loop |
| Cultural and normative adaptation | Cultural adaptation evaluated in 0/45 | No direct paper tests whether social judgements transfer across cultures | None in corpus | Multi-site, multi-culture datasets and adaptation methods | Cross-cultural user studies |
| Privacy and on-device inference | Privacy treated in 1/45; cloud/remote inference 22/45 | Bystander data leaves the robot | PANav; local small VLMs | On-device FMs; privacy-preserving perception | Privacy risk metrics; on-device latency |
| Reproducibility | Code released in 5/45; new datasets released 1/45 | Claims cannot be verified | Code releases (BehAV, LE-Nav, E-SocialNav; DPed-VLN benchmark) | Released code, prompts, model versions and evaluation scripts | Independent replication |
