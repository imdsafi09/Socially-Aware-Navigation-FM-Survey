# T9 - Foundation-model failure modes and mitigations in social navigation

"Evidence type" separates failures directly observed in the corpus from general FM risks relevant to it. Limitation-flag counts are free-text tags from the notes (indicative only).

| Failure mode | Foundation-model cause | Navigation consequence | Evidence in corpus (counts from QUANTITATIVE_FINDINGS) | Mitigation approaches in corpus | Unresolved issue | Evidence type |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Semantic-to-motion grounding gap | FM outputs language/decisions without metric grounding | Decision never executed, or mis-placed goals | 9/45 direct papers never couple FM output to motion; Social-LLaVA uses a hard-coded action mapping; VLM baselines predict image-space goals that must be projected to the metric frame and reach lower pose validity (Group Joining) | Candidate selection (D2), cost layers (D3), grounding modules (G2-Nav, NORM-Nav) | Closed-loop testing of offline decision models | Observed in corpus |
| Hallucination / invalid output | Generative decoding unconstrained by scene | Non-existent obstacles/people; invalid plans | flagged in 4 notes; structural safeguard in 10/45, prompt-level only in 13/45 | Restrict to feasible candidates (Etiquette), bounded output tuples (NORM-Nav), integer ratings (Hey Robot), critics (SAMALM) | No paper measures a hallucination rate in closed loop | Observed (mitigations); rate unmeasured |
| Spatial-relation errors | Weak metric/3D reasoning in VLMs | Wrong side, distance or direction | flagged in 5 notes (e.g., AutoSpatial motivation; Group Joining VLM baselines) | Structured spatial grounding (AutoSpatial), visual prompting with marks (CoNVOI, Etiquette) | Spatial accuracy rarely tested in closed loop | Observed in corpus |
| Human-intention / activity misinterpretation | Ambiguous cues; single-frame context | Inappropriate yield/pass decisions | flagged in 5 notes; intention/activity evaluated (HUM2 Yes) in 2/45 | Multiple hypotheses scored by rollouts (SAATT); temporal context (HCSG) | Inference accuracy seldom measured | Observed in corpus |
| Group-reasoning error | Visual grouping errors | Cutting through groups | flagged in 1 note (GSON grouping errors across LMMs) | Geometric group spaces from FM labels (GSON) | Group evaluation in 3/45 | Observed in corpus |
| Prompt sensitivity | Output depends on prompt wording/framing | Unstable behaviour | flagged in 2 notes (JSAI prompt study) | Prompt design studies; fine-tuning | Robustness not tested on robots | Observed in corpus |
| Temporal inconsistency | Independent per-frame inference | Oscillating decisions | flagged in 2 notes | Memory or temporal context (OLiVia-Nav, HCSG), slow/fast designs | Consistency metrics absent | Observed in corpus |
| Latency / stale reasoning | Seconds-scale FM inference | Acting on outdated scene while people move | flagged in 17 notes; e.g., LISN ~7 s, Sotomi ~20 s, HUMA 2.4 s, MOSU 2.3 s; staleness quantified by SPARK-VLN | Asynchronous slow/fast (9/45), event triggering (HUMA), distillation (D7 8/45), latent streaming | Latency reported numerically in 23/45 only, in non-comparable units | Observed in corpus |
| Cloud / network dependence | API or remote-server inference | Outages, delay, data leaving the robot | flagged in 20 notes; cloud/hybrid/remote inference in 22/45 | Local small VLMs (SNEI family), distillation (Narrate2Nav, OLiVia-Nav) | Offline fallbacks rarely evaluated | Observed in corpus |
| Safety risks in learned or FM-conditioned execution | Learned or FM-conditioned execution with limited independent safety checks | Collisions; no guarantee | FM-independent safety guard in 1/7 direct-action (D6) systems; human-collision rates of ~34% (HUMA, Social-HM3D) and ~29% (SPARK-VLN realistic mode) in D5 systems | Keep a classical planner or CBF/reflex layer (GSON, G2-Nav, Hey Robot) | No shielded VLA in corpus | Observed in corpus |
| Lack of calibrated FM uncertainty | FM outputs carry no calibrated confidence by default | Overconfident social decisions | explicit uncertainty treatment 0/45; partial 9/45 | Sampling / majority vote (PANav), GPR variance (Saito), CVAE sampling (LE-Nav) | No calibrated FM confidence in any direct paper | Observed absence |
| Training-data / annotation bias | Teacher FM or single-annotator labels | Errors baked into students | D7 teacher use in 8/45; SNEI annotations reviewed by one person (Social-LLaVA note); GPT-generated MUSON labels | Human review; multi-action labels (MAction) | Label quality rarely audited | Observed in corpus |
| Cultural / norm bias | Norms learned from pretraining data (raised as a limitation in the corpus) | Norms wrong in other contexts | cultural adaptation evaluated (NOR4 Yes) in 0/45; flagged in 1 note | None in corpus | Entirely open | Raised in corpus; untested |
| Privacy exposure | Off-robot processing of observations or derived context; observing private spaces | Privacy harm to bystanders | privacy treated by 1/45 (PANav); 22/45 papers use off-robot FM inference | Privacy-aware path selection (PANav) | No privacy evaluation of the FM pipeline itself | Partly observed; largely general deployment risk |
