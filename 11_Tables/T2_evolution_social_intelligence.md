# T2 - Evolution of social intelligence in robot navigation

Paradigms overlap; the "Coexistence" column records how each persists inside later systems. Rows marked background cite works outside the coded corpus (see 13_References, background entries). Counts from `final_*` columns (direct n = 45). Source: EVOLUTIONARY_NARRATIVE.md.

| Paradigm | Era (first appearance) | Human representation (Axis A) | Source of social knowledge | Behaviour generation | Representative methods (cite keys) | Main capability gained | Main limitation noted in the literature | Coexistence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Proxemics / spatial theory | 1966 -> (background) | Point with distance zones (A3) | Social science (Hall) | Costs/constraints around people | Hall (background); Kim 2022 group spaces | Explicit comfort distances (SPA1) | Static zones; context and intent not explicitly represented | Thresholds still define FM metrics (PSV, PSC) |
| Social force / rule models | 1995 -> | Agent with desired velocity, anisotropic repulsion, group attraction (A1, A3, A4) | Hand-tuned physical analogy | Force integration / reactive control | Helbing1995SocialForce | Crowd-flow phenomena; simulation | Hand-tuned parameters; semantic context not explicit | SFM is fast loop / simulator in FM systems (LISN, PAD) |
| Geometric collision avoidance | 2008 -> (background) | Moving obstacle (disc, velocity) | Geometry / reciprocity assumptions | Velocity-obstacle optimisation | ORCA/RVO (background); CADRL as learned counterpart | Collision-free reciprocal motion under its reciprocity assumption | Clearance only; social meaning of proximity not explicitly represented | Persists as crowd simulator and baseline |
| Prediction-coupled planning | 2010 -> | Trajectory distribution with interaction potentials (A2, A4) | Probabilistic models fitted to data | Joint inference over predicted futures | Trautman2010UnfreezingIGP | Interaction-aware motion; avoids freezing (HUM1, SPA4) | Computational cost; evaluation on replayed data; norms not explicitly modelled | Prediction modules feed FM systems (Etiquette, HCSG) |
| Inverse RL / imitation | 2012 -> | Feature-based trajectories: clearance, passing side, groups (A2, A4) | Human demonstrations | Planning under learned cost | Kuderer2012FeatureBasedPrediction, Kretzschmar2016SociallyCompliantIRL, Okal2016BayesianIRL, Tai2018GAILDepth | Learned passing / group conventions (SPA2, SPA3); naturalness (EXP3) | Hand-designed features; small demonstration sets | Preference learning reappears with LLM feedback (PREDILECT) |
| Deep RL crowd navigation | 2017 -> | Disc agents with attention over agents (A1, A4) | Reward design + crowd simulation | Learned value / policy | Chen2017CADRL, Chen2017SACADRL, Chen2019SARLCrowdNav | Real-time crowd navigation; norm-shaped rewards | Reward exploitation in simulation; sim-to-real transfer; semantic context not explicit | DRL policies trained or conditioned by FMs (EvoNav, HUMA, SALM) |
| Graph / attention / preference learning | 2019 -> 2024 | Spatio-temporal graphs, embeddings (A1, A4, A6) | Data + human preference feedback | Learned / hybrid policies | Wang2023NaviSTAR, Raj2024HybridSocialNav | Human preference feedback in training; human evaluation of learned policies (EV5) | Geometric representations; context and instructions not explicitly represented | Coexists with FM work |
| Language-conditioned / FM navigation (adjacent) | 2022 -> | Mostly absent (A0 in 11/13) | Web-scale pretraining | LLM planning, value maps, navigation FMs, VLAs | Shah2022LMNav, Huang2023VLMaps, Shah2023ViNT, Yokoyama2024VLFM, Zitkovich2023RT2 | Open-vocabulary grounding; instruction following | No social objective; humans largely absent | Supplies backbones and interfaces (CLIP, VLA, value maps) |
| VLM/LLM social reasoning over classical stacks | 2024 -> | Language descriptions + tracks (A5 30/45, A1 21/45) | Pretrained priors + prompts + fine-tuning (SNEI, MUSON) | FM selects / shapes / sets goals; classical planner executes (D2-D4) or offline decision (D0) | Song2025VLMSocialNav, Sathyamoorthy2024CoNVOI, Luo2025GSON, Chen2026LISN, Liao2026G2Nav, Payandeh2025SocialLLaVA | Context-dependent conduct (NOR3 9/45); instruction following (NOR2 8/45); explanation (R5) | Seconds-scale latency reported in several systems; cloud dependence; 9/45 never coupled to motion; little human evaluation | Most frequent pattern in the corpus |
| Distilled, latent and VLA social navigation | 2025 -> | Implicit latents (A6 15/45) | FM as teacher or backbone; IL/RL post-training | Training-time supervision (D7), latent conditioning (D5), direct action (D6) | Payandeh2026Narrate2Nav, Elnoor2026ViLAM, Ahmadi2026HUMA, Hu2026SPARKVLN, Chen2026SocialNavCVPR, Wang2026SALSA | Real-time execution without per-step FM calls; end-to-end social VLAs | Limited interpretability; FM-independent safety in 1/7 D6 systems; D6 robot evidence 2/7 | Newest; overlaps with the preceding paradigm |
